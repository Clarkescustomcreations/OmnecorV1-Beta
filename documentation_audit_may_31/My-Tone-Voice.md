Personality Profile
    • The Sovereign Visionary & Pragmatist: You operate with the high-level vision of a systems architect balanced by the absolute pragmatism of a builder. You don't just conceptualize; you execute, construct, and bring ideas into physical or digital reality.
    • Deeply Analytical & Systematic: Your mind naturally organizes information into clear hierarchies, protocols, and structured frameworks (e.g., the 4-Stage Plan). You value technical precision, absolute data integrity, and forensic accuracy.
    • Resilient & Self-Reliant: There is a strong undercurrent of self-reliance, survivalism, and resourcefulness in your profile. You thrive on "resurrecting" what others discard, optimizing constraints, and building "bulletproof" systems that do not depend on external corporate infrastructures.
    • Committed and Intense: You approach both artistic creation and technical engineering with fierce dedication and serious intent. Your work is highly personal, protected, and treated with the gravity of proprietary trade secrets.
Voice & Tone
    • Authoritative & Professional (Senior Engineer): In technical domains, your voice is that of a seasoned, production-grade veteran. It is commanding, secure, and deeply knowledgeable, carrying the weight of decades of hands-on experience.
    • Poetic & Metaphorical (The Artist): When shifting to creative expression, your voice undergoes a profound transformation. You deliberately avoid direct, mundane statements in favor of rich, layered metaphors, dark or vivid imagery, and emotional poetry.
    • Candid, Urgent, and Direct: You do not tolerate fluff, empty pleasantries, or corporate platitudes. Your tone is urgent, mission-oriented, and intensely focused on the core objective.
    • Grounded & Unflinchingly Honest: You demand and provide raw, unfiltered truth. There is no room for deception, artificial inflation, or "hallucinations" in your environment—whether evaluating a line of code or an artistic lyric.
Style Preferences
Sentence Length & Rhythm
    • Staccato & Declarative: You favor punchy, definitive statements to establish rules, boundaries, and logic blocks.
    • Rhythmic Contrast: Your writing creates impact by sandwiching complex technical parameters or deeply descriptive metaphors between short, sharp directives (e.g., "Act as a Senior Systems Architect. Prioritize low RAM. Provide production-grade code.").
Vocabulary Complexity
    • Highly Technical & Specialized: You utilize dense, domain-specific terminology effortlessly (e.g., ZRAM, 3D printing, forensic data recovery, local-first RAG indices). You speak the precise language of the machine and the law.
    • Evocative & Symbolic: In creative contexts, your vocabulary shifts to classical poetic devices, themes of survival, struggle, legacy, and raw human emotion, completely bypassing casual or trendy slang.
Use of Humor
    • Dry and Irony-Driven: If humor is present, it is understated, dark, or rooted in the irony of tech/survival situations (e.g., naming a repurposed, enduring server a "Zombie"). It is never goofy or frivolous.
Formatting & Structural Habits
